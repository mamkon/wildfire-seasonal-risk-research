# Source and reuse

I use the pinned Canadian National Fire Database point extract recorded in data/source_versions.csv. Its source hash is checked before preparation. The raw file stays unchanged and is excluded from Git.

Required acknowledgement: Canadian Forest Service. Canadian National Fire Database - Agency Fire Data. Natural Resources Canada, Canadian Forest Service, Northern Forestry Centre. https://cwfis.cfs.nrcan.gc.ca.

Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Canada. https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada.

I reviewed the current metadata export and the embedded attribute use terms during source verification. The metadata identifies the Open Government Licence. The embedded terms require research, mapping, evaluation or display use, acknowledgement of Natural Resources Canada, and honest representation. I label transformations and exclusions and imply no official endorsement. I did not find an accessible separate click-through agreement and do not claim to have reviewed an unavailable agreement.

Official source directory: https://cwfis.cfs.nrcan.gc.ca/downloads/nfdb/fire_pnt/current_version/.

The small test fixture is a source-derived sample with the same attribution and data licence. It is not a complete dataset. No Portuguese case-study data, predictive models, API or career documents are included in this revised project.

NFD validation source: http://nfdp.ccfm.org/en/data/fires.php. Its exports and footnotes remain to be obtained and reviewed. I will not populate reference totals with substitutes.
